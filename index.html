Details: goal, adaptation, checks, provenance

Scripted episodes, one per task: object motion and task events follow the clip; the embodiment, fixtures and plunger actuation are adapted and listed under each task. Checks are computed from simulator state and shown as measured. No liquid is simulated.

Human clips are shown under their own licences (LabOS, LSV CC-BY-NC-4.0, CyberOrigin MIT) for research comparison. Robot models: MuJoCo Menagerie (UR5e, Robotiq 2F-85, Wonik Allegro). Labware: Opentrons definitions (Apache-2.0). Hand synergies: EigenDexplore (MIT).
